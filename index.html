Hey there!

I have recently completed the Erasmus Mundus Master in Quantum Technologies and Engineering (QuanTEEM), which took me through Aarhus University in Denmark, RPTU Kaiserslautern in Germany, and Université Bourgogne Europe in France.

My research experience spans ultrafast pump–probe spectroscopy (and coherent phonon spectroscopy), femtosecond lasers, ultrafast dynamics in quantum materials, quantum many-body theory, phase transitions in lattices and frustrated magnetism, optical lattices, and high-energy physics.

I am currently looking for a PhD position in the interplay between condensed matter and light-matter interactions, exploring topics such as strongly correlated phenomena in solids, ultrafast dynamics in quantum materials, optical lattices and quantum simulators. I am also eager to collaborate in cutting-edge R&D projects within these fields, contributing to push the boundaries of our domain in quantum science and its applications to new technologies.

My research interest in condensed matter was inspired by the emergent phenomena in quantum many-body systems. During my Bachelor's studies at the National University of Colombia, I trained in high-energy physics theory and cosmology, working out a thermal leptogenesis model for heavy neutrinos and QCD axions as candidates for dark matter. But it was during the "2022 Perimeter-SAIFR-IFT Journeys into Theoretical Physics" school, which I attended at IFT/ICTP-SAIFR in São Paulo, Brazil, that I changed my research interests to condensed and quantum matter. Although my background has been mainly theoretical, my passion for quantum mechanics and light–matter interaction has led me to engage with experimental physics.

Research experience

Jan – Jun 2026

Ultrafast dynamics and light-induced phase transitions in tellurium

Associate Research Student · Ultrafast Dynamics of Quantum Materials Group, Aarhus University — Supervisor: Prof. Simon E. Wall

  • Carried out table-top pump–probe and rotational-anisotropy spectroscopy in the near-infrared, under ultra-high vacuum and cryogenic conditions.
  • Extracted coherent phonon frequencies, amplitudes and damping rates from linear and non-linear transient reflectance data by fitting time-domain signals to Raman-active phonons in the Displacive-Excitation of Coherent Phonons (DECP) model.
  • Skilled in second-harmonic generation and sum-frequency generation in crystals on free-space optical setups.
  • Applied group symmetry techniques to the dielectric function and the non-linear susceptibility tensor for arbitrarily oriented crystals, modelling mathematically the Raman modulation of the optical response, and characterising phonon spectra and optical anisotropy.
  • Reviewed ultrafast science, and non-equilibrium dynamics in lattices undergoing a light-induced phase transition.
  • Pump–probe spectroscopy
  • Femtosecond lasers
  • Coherent phonons
  • Ultrafast dynamics
  • Second-harmonic generation
  • Rotational anisotropy
  • Cryogenics and UHV
  • Data acquisition and signal processing

Spin-wave theory for frustrated Heisenberg antiferromagnets

Research Intern · Center for Complex Quantum Systems, Aarhus University — Supervisor: Prof. Georg Bruun

  • Derived analytic dispersion and magnetisation expressions for nearest- and next-nearest-neighbour Heisenberg antiferromagnets on arbitrary lattice geometries.

  • Built Python tools for magnon dispersion and regularised Brillouin-zone integration, benchmarking staggered magnetisation against quantum Monte Carlo and series-expansion results.
